# Jannyca-app

A Laravel application whose current deliverable is a single landing page introducing **Jannyca Ilaida**, a BSIT student from Trimex. The page is served at the root route as the `welcome` view.

## People

- **Jannyca Ilaida** — the person the page introduces. A girl/woman; a BSIT student at Trimex. The brief requires the page to read soft, feminine, and "girlie," and to be visually distinct from a prior dark split-screen design made for someone else.

## The pinned brief (do not dilute)

- One-page personal introduction, no concept roll. The brief is precise and narrow; the surface was built directly under it.
- World: satin and stage light on warm ivory program stock, one committed ballet rose carrying the second name line, the rules, and a drawn satin bow; hairline rules; Didone star type (Bodoni Moda).
- Motion: one authored entry choreography with exponential ease-out, with a full `prefers-reduced-motion` kill switch.
- The visitor should read a name, not a dashboard: the page is a single printed "program bill" sheet, not a three-card intro grid.
